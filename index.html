01 / ABOUT

From fundamental physics
to functional technology.

I connect advanced logic devices, III-nitride nanomaterials, and light-driven energy conversion.

I am a Device Engineer in the Advanced Logic Technology Platform at IBM Research, Albany. My work spans 2 nm gate-all-around nanosheet FET technology, multiple-Vt solutions for high performance and low power, and eFuse development for advanced CMOS nodes.

My doctoral research with Professor Zetian Mi at the University of Michigan explored epitaxial semiconductor nanostructures for artificial photosynthesis, solar water splitting, and optoelectronics. My earlier research with Professor Samia Subrina at BUET investigated thermal and electronic transport in low-dimensional materials using molecular dynamics and density functional theory.

I bring hands-on experience from approximately 1,000 molecular beam epitaxy growths, cleanroom fabrication, materials and device characterization, and computational modeling, alongside university teaching and research mentoring.

01 / ADVANCED LOGIC

Semiconductor device engineering

2 nm GAA nanosheet FET research and development, with multiple-Vt solutions for high-performance and low-power applications. Device optimization connects simulation, fabrication, electrical and physical characterization, and statistical analysis.

02 / SOLAR FUELS

Artificial photosynthesis

GaN/InGaN nanowire photocatalysts for high-efficiency, long-term solar water splitting: epitaxial growth, reaction-condition optimization, and protective architectures. Related work includes InGaN/Si tandem photocathodes, co-catalyst engineering, CO₂ reduction, and dilute Bi-doped nitrides for solar fuels.

GaN / InGaNSolar water splittingMBE
03 / OPTOELECTRONICS

III-nitride nanostructures

MBE-grown GaN/InGaN micro-network deep-nano structures for optoelectronics and photocatalysis, including optical characterization and carrier dynamics. Additional work explores N-polar GaN/InGaN/AlGaN green micro-LEDs on silicon through selective-area growth, polarization, and strain engineering.

Deep-nano structuresCharacterizationEpitaxy
04 / COMPUTATIONAL MATERIALS

Thermal & electronic transport

Molecular dynamics studies of pristine and defect-induced graphene nanoribbons and strain-engineered hexagonal boron nitride. DFT studies examine electronic properties of pristine and carbon/silicon-doped stanene, linking composition and structure to thermal and electronic behavior.

Molecular dynamicsDFT2D materials
05 / MEMORY & RELIABILITY

eFuse & one-time programmable memory

At IBM Research, I work on eFuse development for advanced CMOS technology nodes. From a permanent change in the fuse link to a readable memory state: programmable devices, resistance sensing, and applications in memory repair, trimming, and configuration. Conceptual illustration.
